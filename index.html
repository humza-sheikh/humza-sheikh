02 The story

It started with a spreadsheet, a PayPal account, and a 13-year-old who refused to wait for an allowance. I was sourcing Xbox accessories from Chinese manufacturers and selling them on eBay UK before I could drive.

What that first business taught me

  1. i.Supplier negotiation is a conversation, not a transaction.
  2. ii.Margins live in the details.
  3. iii.The internet doesn't care how old you are if you can deliver value.
